## Method

1. **YouTube search (waves 1–2, recovered)**: yt-dlp `ytsearchN:` flat-playlist searches — season-specific queries (2007–2026), rivalries (Fenerbahçe/Galatasaray/Beşiktaş derbies), UEFA/European nights, cup finals, championship moments, broadcaster channels (beIN SPORTS Türkiye/Arşiv, TRT), Turkish terms ("gol", "maç özeti", "şampiyonluk", "kadro", "anlatım").
2. **Enrichment (this run)**: per-video `yt-dlp --dump-json --skip-download` for high-confidence records → upload_date, duration, license field, description. Creative Commons license flag promoted rights_confidence to high.
3. **Non-YouTube Turkish sources (this run)**: trtarsiv.com (TRT 1968–2015 video archive, streaming only, no download; audio archive explicitly not yet included), TRT Radyo 1 / Radyospor 97.5 FM Trabzon (live match commentary — stream only, no archive), tff.org (official fact source + TFF YouTube channel), trabzonspor.org.tr (club archive), trtspor.com.tr, ntvspor.net, fanatik.com.tr, hurriyet.com.tr/sporarena, sabah.com.tr/spor, aa.com.tr (fact verification sources). Recorded as archive/portal records with media_type and access_status; no English-language sources cited.
4. **Match facts**: dates/scores parsed from video titles (beIN SPORTS Türkiye titles follow a reliable "DD.MM.YYYY | Team-Team | N-N" pattern). Citable fact sources for cross-check: tff.org, aa.com.tr, fanatik.com.tr, ntvspor.net. NOT verified by playback — playback verification is the next lane's job.

## Feasibility note for the >1,000 verified clips target

This census covers one era lane (2007–2026) and yields 448 high-confidence source *videos* (not clips). Many are 4–10 minute highlight packages from which multiple short moment clips (goals, penalties, crowd reactions, final whistles) can legitimately be cut IF rights permit. The 42 CC-licensed videos plus any future authorized routes are the only currently lawful extraction base. Reaching >1,000 verified clips across all eras will require: (a) rights authorization or CC-only extraction policy, (b) sibling-era censuses (1967–1986 delivered 18 records in t_8a57228a; 1987–2006 lane separate), (c) per-moment timestamp verification via transcripts/playback. **Do not count search results as clips.** Honest assessment: source-video supply exists (1,945 records here), but legally retainable clip count is gated by rights, not discovery.

## Limitations

1. Match dates/scores come from title parsing, not playback/transcript verification (176/1,945 have dates; beIN-pattern titles are highly reliable but unverified).
2. "official_broadcaster" rights tag = uploader identity evidence, NOT a reuse license. No audio extraction was attempted anywhere rights were unclear — URLs recorded instead, per task instruction.
3. trtarsiv.com content is not externally searchable; on-site browsing is required to enumerate Trabzonspor match recordings (portal recorded, items not enumerated).
4. Radio commentary exists only as live streams (TRT Radyo 1) — no public archive; past-match radio audio is effectively unavailable.
5. Dedupe is by YouTube video ID; the same match may appear from multiple uploaders (not deduped — intentional, different sources carry different rights).
6. 9 raw records lack duration (flat-search gaps); low-confidence set has no upload dates (enrichment budget reserved for high-confidence per time limit).
